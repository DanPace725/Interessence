# Rune Formation Dynamics

**Runehood is an earned property, not a label.**
An inscription becomes a Rune only when it survives the **Stress Test Protocol** and achieves **Closure Stability**.

---

## 1. The Stress Test Protocol

To determine if a composition is stable enough to be a Rune, it must be tested against perturbation.

### Test A: Domain Shift (Context)
*Does the signal hold its logic when the substrate changes?*
-   **Magic Context**: Inscribed in mana-conductive stone.
-   **Biological Context**: Encoded in a protein chain.
-   **Machine Context**: Running as logic gates in a circuit.
-   **Criterion**: The **emergent behavior** must be analogous across all domains. (e.g., "Binding" must function as a lock, a bond, and an anchor).

### Test B: Scale Shift (Resolution)
*Does the signal hold when resolution changes?*
-   **Macro**: Seen from a distance (low detail).
-   **Micro**: Seen with high precision.
-   **Criterion**: The "General Shape" of the signal bias must remain recognizable. It should not look like "Fire" at a distance and "Water" up close.

### Test C: Observer Shift (Epistemic)
*Does the signal converge across mismatched observers?*
-   **Test**: Subject the inscription to the [Epistemic Traversal Logic](Epistemic_Traversal_Logic.md) errors (Blindness, Inversion).
-   **Criterion**: Does the inscription force observers to move (Traversal) towards a single shared truth? Or does it allow them to happily exist in separate, contradictory realities?
-   **Rune Condition**: A Rune **compels convergence**. It creates enough friction that observers *must* resolve the ambiguity.

---

## 2. Emergence Criteria

### Basin Depth (Resistance to Perturbation)
How much energy is required to "break" the closure?
-   **Shallow Basin (Inscription)**: A typo, a breeze, or a weak counter-signal disrupts the effect.
-   **Deep Basin (Rune)**: Requires significant energy to disrupt. The system "wants" to snap back to this state.

### Hysteresis (Memory)
Does the system remember the state after the pressure is removed?
-   **Elastic (Inscription)**: Signal stops -> Effect vanishes immediately.
-   **Plastic (Rune)**: Signal stops -> Effect persists (Remanence).

---

## 3. Failure Modes (Why things fail to become Runes)

### Divergence (The "Tower of Babel" Effect)
The composition looks stable locally, but observers from different angles see fundamentally incompatible truths that never resolve. The system splits into non-interacting realities.

### Decay (Entropy)
The composition requires constant active maintenance (Signal injection) to exist. As soon as attention/mana is withdrawn, it dissolves back into noise.
*Runes are low-entropy states that self-sustain (or decay very slowly).*

---

## 4. Game Logic Implementation

**For Designers/AI:**
1.  **Do not** manually tag items as "Runes."
2.  **Do** simulate the history of the inscription.
    -   Has it been used 1000 times? (Reinforcement)
    -   Has it worked in the forest and the cave? (Domain stability)
    -   Do players agree on what it does? (Observer convergence)
3.  **If Yes**: Promote `Inscription` → `Rune`. (Unlock persistence/efficiency bonuses).
