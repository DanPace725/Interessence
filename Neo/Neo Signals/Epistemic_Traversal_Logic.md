# Epistemic Traversal Logic

**How observer position creates locally valid but globally incompatible interpretations.**

In a 3D inscription system, "Truth" is only accessible via **Traversal** (moving to seeing all sides). Static observers are subject to **Perceptual Error**.

---

## 1. The Projection Matrix

This matrix defines what an Observer **Perceives** (Input) given the **True Glyph** (Reality) and their **Viewpoint**.

| **True Glyph** | **Viewpoint: Right Face (A)** | **Viewpoint: Left Face (B)** | **Epistemic State** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **B-Group** (Right) | **Perceives: B-Group** (Correct) | **Perceives: Silence** (Empty) | **Asymmetric Blindness** |
| **H-Group** (Left) | **Perceives: Silence** (Empty) | **Perceives: H-Group** (Correct) | **Asymmetric Blindness** |

## 2. Conflict Dynamics (Signal Friction)

When two characters/processes act on the same glyph from different sides, their mismatched perceptions create specific types of signal friction.

### A. Divergent Misidentification (The Cross Problem)
**Scenario**: Users act on a **Muin (M)** glyph.
-   **Right Observer**: Reads "Beith" (Initiate).
-   **Left Observer**: Reads "Huath" (React).
-   **Result**: The system receives two incomplete, seemingly unrelated local signals instead of the true "Bind" signal.
-   **Outcome**: The spell fails or creates a "Tearing" effect, as one pulls and the other pushes, rather than binding together.

### B. Inverted Polarity (The Diagonal Problem)
**Scenario**: Users act on an **Ailm (A)** glyph.
-   **Right Observer**: Reads "Forward Flow".
-   **Left Observer**: Reads "Reverse Flow" (because the slash / looks like \ from behind).
-   **Result**: complete destructive interference. Two vectors push against each other.
-   **Outcome**: Deadlock or Explosion (if magnitude is high).

### C. Asymmetric Blindness (The Side Problem)
**Scenario**: Users act on a **Beith (B)** glyph.
-   **Right Observer**: "I see a signal."
-   **Left Observer**: "There is no signal."
-   **Result**: Disagreement on reality. One acts, the other hesitates.
-   **Outcome**: Loss of synchronization. The "Blind" observer breaks the cooperative circuit by failing to maintain signal continuity.

---

## 3. Resolution: Traversal

The only way to resolve these conflicts is **Traversal** (Movement).

-   **Parallax**: Moving 15-45 degrees reveals the *depth* of the cut, distinguishing a Cross (M) from a Side (B).
-   **Circumnavigation**: Moving 180 degrees reveals the Inversion error.
-   **Communication**: "I see Beith" + "I see Huath" = Logic Deduction -> "It must be Muin."

**Game Mechanic**:
High-level cooperative magic requires players to constantly communicate their partial views to triangulate the True Glyph, effectively solving a distributed geometric puzzle in real-time.
