# Glyph Signal Compendium

**Derived via [Signal Definition Protocol](Signal_Definition_Protocol.md)**

This document catalogs the structural signal bias for every NeOgham glyph.
*Note: "Magnitude" refers to mark count (1-5).*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Usage Note: Bias vs. Determinism**
> The verbs used below (e.g., "Forces", "Negates", "Dominates") describe **probabilistic bias**, not deterministic overrides.
> -   **"Forces"** → Makes alternative closures extremely unlikely.
> -   **"Negates"** → Dampens or reroutes signal intensity.
> -   **"Dominates"** → Overwhelms local modulation capacity.
> -   **"Primary/Secondary"** → Frame-relative terms, not absolute ontological roles.
>
> No glyph universally overrides the Global Closure Operator (GCO) or ignores constraints. They bias the landscape, they do not dictate the outcome.
