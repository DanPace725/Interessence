# Pairwise Interference Logic

**Methodology for calculating interactions between any two NeOgham glyphs.**

Instead of memorizing 625 combinations, use this logic to derive the emergent signal of a pair (G1 + G2).

---

## 1. Magnitude Interaction (Intensity)

Compare the Magnitude (Mark Count 1-5) of the two glyphs.
Let $\Delta M = |M_1 - M_2|$.

| Condition | Rule Name | Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Equal** ($M_1 == M_2$) | **Resonance** | Both signals amplify. The resulting effect is stronger than the sum of parts. Stability increases. |
| **Close** ($\Delta M = 1$) | **Modulation** | The stronger glyph drives, but is "flavored" or "colored" by the weaker one. |
| **Disparate** ($\Delta M \ge 2$) | **Dominance** | The stronger glyph overwrites the weaker one. The weaker glyph acts merely as a localized modifier or "adjective" to the strong glyph's "noun". |

---

## 2. Aicme Interaction Matrix (Structure)

Determine the structural interaction based on the Groups (Aicme) of the pair.

| | **Right (B)** | **Left (H)** | **Cross (M)** | **Diag (A)** | **Inverse (F)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Right (B)** | **Reinforce** <br> (Sum Vector) | **Opposition** <br> (Tension/Balance) | **Anchor** <br> (Lock State) | **Propel** <br> (Vectorize) | **Distort** <br> (Mutate) |
| **Left (H)** | **Opposition** <br> (Tension/Balance) | **Reinforce** <br> (Sum Inverse) | **Anchor** <br> (Lock State) | **Dampen** <br> (Resist Flow) | **Invert** <br> (Flip Polarity) |
| **Cross (M)** | **Anchor** <br> (Lock State) | **Anchor** <br> (Lock State) | **Crystalize** <br> (Rigid Structure) | **Gate** <br> (Control Flow) | **Fracture** <br> (Break Bond) |
| **Diag (A)** | **Propel** <br> (Vectorize) | **Dampen** <br> (Resist Flow) | **Gate** <br> (Control Flow) | **Stream** <br> (Laminar Flow) | **Turbulence** <br> (Chaos/Mix) |
| **Inverse (F)** | **Distort** <br> (Mutate) | **Invert** <br> (Flip Polarity) | **Fracture** <br> (Break Bond) | **Turbulence** <br> (Chaos/Mix) | **Phase Shift** <br> (Transmute) |

---

### Matrix Definitions

-   **Reinforce**: Inputs align. Signal intensity sums cleanly.
-   **Opposition**: Inputs conflict. Creates high "Potential Energy" (Tension) or cancels out (Balance) depending on Magnitude.
-   **Anchor**: One glyph attempts to freeze or bind the other to a location/state.
-   **Propel/Dampen**: Interaction with Flow vectors. Right moves with flow; Left resists it.
-   **Gate**: The Cross structure acts as a valve for the Diagonal flow.
-   **Distort/Turbulence**: Interaction with Inverse vectors. Predictability creates noise or mutation.

---

## 3. Example Derivations

**Test A: Beith (B1) + Huath (H1)**
-   **Mag**: Equal (1 vs 1) -> **Resonance**.
-   **Matrix**: Right + Left -> **Opposition**.
-   **Result**: **Resonant Tension**. A stable, vibrating state of balance. High potential, zero movement. "A battery."

**Test B: Peith (P5) + Beith (B1)**
-   **Mag**: Disparate (5 vs 1) -> **Dominance**. P5 rules.
-   **Matrix**: Right + Right -> **Reinforce**.
-   **Result**: **Driving Force (P)** slightly flavored by **Initiation (B)**. The massive drive has a specific "start" flavor, but Beith is overwhelmed.

**Test C: Tinne (H3) + Ur (A3)**
-   **Mag**: Equal (3 vs 3) -> **Resonance**.
-   **Matrix**: Left + Diag -> **Dampen**.
-   **Result**: **Resonant Resistance**. The flow (Ur) is effectively blocked by the shield (Tinne). A "Dam" or "Shield" effect.

**Test D: Straif (Z4) + Ui (F3)**
-   **Mag**: Close (4 vs 3) -> **Modulation**. Z4 leads.
-   **Matrix**: Cross + Inverse -> **Fracture**.
-   **Result**: **Fractured Anchor**. The binding (Z) is breaking apart (F). A "Trap responding to trigger" or "Crumbling Wall".
