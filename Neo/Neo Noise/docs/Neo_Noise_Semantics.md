## 3. Interpretation Rules

### Rule 1: The Principle of Bias
> *The noise does not describe **what** is there, but **how** reality behaves there.*

*   **Void (Low Density)**: Chasms, empty plains, magic-dead zones, amnesia.
*   **Singularity (High Density)**: Floating islands, dungeons, revelation.

### Rule 2: The Gradient of Truth (Volatility)
Rapid transitions describe **Volatility**.
*   **Smooth Gradient**: A stable evolution (High Stability Score). Safe to traverse.
*   **Hard Edge**: A "Truth Shear" (Low Stability Score). An immediate transition implies violence.

### Rule 3: The Source of Pressure
The high values (Singularity) are generated by specific interactions:
1.  **Anchoring (Cross + Cross)**: The "White" spots in `STONE` are points of **immovable weight**.
2.  **Phase-Shifting (Backslash + Backslash)**: The "White" spots in `CHAOS_THEORY` are points of **mutation**.

*You cannot distinguish them by color alone (both are white), but you know they come from different sources based on the Inscription.*

## 4. Universal Key
**"The brighter the pixel, the more 'Real' specific local reality is."**

*   **Darkness** = Ambiguity, Freedom, Nullity.
*   **Brightness** = Definition, Constraint, Existence.
