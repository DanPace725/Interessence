Neo Rotation Atlas

A first-pass visual model for directed glyph rotation. Neo's marks live in a top-biased five-slot lattice, so a rotated glyph can become shape-similar to another glyph while its origin, stem direction, stroke order, and arrow direction remain visible as residue.

Shape The nearest screen-space family after rotation, ignoring stroke direction and mark order.
Residue The preserved identity: original letter, rotation, stem direction, stroke vector path, and mark index order.
Highlight Gold borders mark exact primary shape matches whose residue is not canonical.
Hypothesis Projection equivalence is not inscriptional identity. Rotation produces a derived glyph-state.
