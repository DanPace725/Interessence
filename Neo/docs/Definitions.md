# Neo System Definitions

## Hierarchy of Form

### 1. Glyph
*The Atomic Unit.*
A single, indivisible mark in the NeOgham 5x5 grid.
-   **Function**: Carries a specific, localized signal bias. It is a "field-shaping primitive."
-   **Semantics**: Has no complete meaning on its own. Non-referential.
-   **Properties**: Defined by Orientation, Magnitude (mark count), and Position (aicme).

### 2. Inscription
*The Composition.*
A sequence or arrangement of Glyphs (analogous to a word or sentence).
-   **Function**: Explores the signal space. Creates complex patterns of bias.
-   **Stability**: Unstable. Its meaning is probabilistic and highly context-dependent.
-   **Dynamics**: Subject to interpretation errors, "typos," and misreads.

### 3. Rune
*The Stabilized Unit.*
An Inscription that has undergone repeated **Global Closure** (GCO) and stabilized into a distinct, persistent unit.
-   **Function**: Acts as a "macro" or a deep basin in the signal field.
-   **Origin**: Runes are *earned*, not designed. They emerge from history and repetition.
-   **Properties**: "More than the sum of its parts" due to historical reinforcement.

---

## Core Mechanisms

### Signal
A continuous "pressure" or bias field that permeates the game world.
-   Glyphs and Inscriptions modulate this field.
-   It is the medium through which influence propagates.

### Global Closure Operator (GCO)
The system's "collapse function."
-   Converts continuous Signal pressure into discrete reality (state changes).
-   Runes are inscriptions that reliably survive GCO.

### Embodiment
The requirement of physical presence and movement to access information.
-   Because Glyphs are 3D (carved on edges), a static observer cannot see the whole picture.
-   Understanding requires time and energy expenditure (navigation).
