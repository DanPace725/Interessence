Field Reading — FRICTION

The word opens with F (Median Assertion) — a steady, founding pressure. Not explosive, just consistent. R (Maximal Fusion) immediately fires at full magnitude via Dominance, anchoring hard and collapsing distinctions.

I (Maximal Jet) meets R at equal magnitude. The Gate/Resonance between them means the Jet passes through amplified — the field is at peak kinetic energy. Then it hits C (Strong Barrier). The I→C interaction — Dampen / Modulation — is the structural heart of the word. Maximum velocity meeting deep resistance. Not destroyed, but flavored and slowed.

The barrier C and the balance T reinforce each other (both left-group), holding the resistance in place. Then IO (Maximal Chaos) arrives and inverts T's equilibrium by Dominance. The field collapses inward. IO then fractures the terminal N (Median Lock) — but the Lock persists. FRICTION closes as a locked, fractured state. It doesn't resolve. It freezes.

Structural Profile

Across both words, 11 glyphs fire in sequence. Four reach magnitude 5 — the maximal tier — making this a high-energy phrase. The phrase contains 3 Fracture interactions (IO→N, OI→N, and structurally at the boundary), more than any other interaction type. Fracture is the phrase's dominant event.

The cross-group (N, R) appears three times and both times serves as an absorbing node — receiving high-energy signals (Maximal Jet, Maximal Chaos, Maximal Drive) and holding. This is the structural backbone: friction creates a locked node; point drives into it.

The lone Dampen / Modulation at I→C is the quietest interaction in the phrase — and the most significant. It is where energy meets resistance and is transformed rather than destroyed. Everything before it (F→R→I) builds kinetic charge. Everything after it (C→T→IO→N) manages and locks the aftermath.

Composite Reading

A Friction Point is a field event in three acts. In the first, energy is charged to maximum — a Median Assertion grows through Maximal Fusion into a Maximal Jet, amplified at resonance through a Gate. In the second, that Jet meets a Strong Barrier and is dampened — not destroyed, but transformed. The resistance holds and reinforces itself. Then chaos inverts the stabilizers and fractures the Lock. The field freezes.

In the third act, a Maximal Drive arrives and anchors itself to the frozen state with Dominance. The Point does not dissolve the friction. It plants itself in it. The word "POINT" closes in resonant equilibrium — stable, arrived, settled — precisely because the locked, fractured state beneath it gave the drive something to anchor against.

In NEO terms: a Friction Point is the place where maximum kinetic energy is dampened into a locked state, and a new maximum drive anchors itself to that lock. The friction doesn't end. The point just holds it still.
