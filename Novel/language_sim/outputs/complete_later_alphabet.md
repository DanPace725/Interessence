# Complete Later IW Alphabet

This is the later analytical alphabet: 10 consonant mouth-family letters and 5 vowel-field letters.

It is not the primitive root language. Each letter is a fossilized mouth-operation abstracted from older embodied sound-events, gestures, postures, and survival contexts.

The body speaks first. The alphabet is what remains after generations of mouths, hands, and stones agree on a pattern.

## Consonant Mouth-Families

| Name | Letter | Dual phonetics | Gesture | Brief meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fáevá | `FV` | `f / v (light/deep)` | lower lip brushes the teeth while the hand filters outward | filtering, softening, friction, warning breath |
| Píeb | `PB` | `p / b (light/deep)` | closed fist opens from the mouth line | beginning, burst, permission, opening pressure |
| Sílezá | `SZ` | `s / z (light/deep)` | two fingers hover at the throat while breath narrows | listening, warning, waiting, surface pressure |
| Théá | `TH` | `th / dh (light/deep)` | two fingers hold at the lip edge before turning away | threshold, taboo edge, named limit, tested safety |
| Detrá | `DT` | `t / d (light/deep)` | two fingers tap forward and release | strike, command, work rhythm, disciplined release |
| Kirga | `KG` | `k / g (light/deep)` | cupped hand closes inward near the ribs | carrying, cutting, holding, grounded oath |
| Mená | `MN` | `m / n (position)` | hand closes over the chest, then releases through the nose | memory, flesh, obligation, inside continuity |
| Léár | `LR` | `l / r (routing)` | finger traces a bending path around an obstacle | path, lineage, river, flexible law |
| Chasha | `CHSH` | `ch / sh (duration/release)` | flat hand scrapes forward, then folds downward | scrape, cooling cover, released danger, ritual hush |
| Hala | `H` | `h / hh (breath phase)` | open palm lowers as breath leaves the body | hush, venting, prohibition, sacred boundary |

## Vowel Fields

| Name | Letter | Dual phonetics | Gesture | Brief meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alehá | `A` | `a / aa (duration)` | open palm and open chest | open field, presence, release |
| Elír | `E` | `e / ee (duration)` | two fingers narrow toward a point | attention, sorting, careful focus |
| Íríel | `I` | `i / ii (duration)` | one finger draws a thin upward line | line, signal, fine path |
| Ómáú | `O` | `o / oo (duration)` | hand rounds into a small chamber | containment, chamber, safe passage |
| Urá | `U` | `u / uu (duration)` | hand curves inward and down | depth, return, inner channel |

## Reading Rules

- The written letter names the mouth-operation, not a single fixed sound.
- `FV`, `PB`, `SZ`, `TH`, `DT`, and `KG` use light/deep contrast: breath-light versus body/throat-deep.
- `MN`, `LR`, `CHSH`, and `H` use position, routing, release/duration, or breath-phase contrast.
- Vowel letters use short/held duration as their dual realization.
- `w` is not a consonant-family letter in this stage. It behaves as a glide that emerges around vowel pairs.
